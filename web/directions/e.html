Three channels. Zero cloud.

Everything runs on your machine. The mixing board never phones home.

Channel 01

100% on-device

Whisper runs locally on Apple silicon. Your voice never leaves the machine — no API keys, no bill, works offline. Optional AI cleanup stays local via Ollama.

Channel 02

Meetings, transcribed

Records your mic and the call's audio, then labels who said what — You / Others — so the minutes cut themselves while you actually listen.

Channel 03

Speak Hindi, type English

13 languages, including Hindi transliterated to Roman script. Bolo Hindi mein, it types it just like that. Code-switch mid-sentence; it keeps up.
