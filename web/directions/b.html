Three tracks. No B-sides.

Track 01

100% on-device

Whisper runs locally on your Mac. Your voice never leaves the machine: no API keys, no cloud, no bill. Optional local AI cleanup via Ollama.

Track 02

Meetings, transcribed

Records both sides of a call and labels who said what (You / Others), so the minutes write themselves while you actually listen.

Track 03

Speak Hindi, type English

13 languages, including Hindi transliterated to Roman script. Bolo Hindi mein, likha jaayega waise hi. Code-switch freely.
