Why OpenWispr

Private by architecture.

100% on-device

Whisper runs on the Apple Neural Engine. Your voice never touches a server; works fully offline. Optional AI cleanup stays local via Ollama.

Meetings transcribed

Captures your mic and the call's system audio (Zoom, Meet, Teams) and labels who said what, split by You / Others. Export as Markdown.

Speak Hindi, type English

13 languages, auto-detected or pinned. Speak in Hindi and OpenWispr types it in Roman script. Code-switch mid-sentence, it keeps up.
