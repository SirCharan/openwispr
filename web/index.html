How it works

Three steps to your first dictation.

Download & open

Grab the app, drag it to Applications, right-click → Open the first time.

Grant access

Allow Microphone and Accessibility when the setup wizard asks.

Dictate

Hold ⌘⇧D, speak, release — your text pastes at the cursor.

First open: because this build isn't notarized yet, macOS Gatekeeper blocks a double-click. Right-click Whispr.app → Open → Open once, and it runs normally after that.
