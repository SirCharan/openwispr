Feature reel

Six tracks. No B-sides.

Track 01

100% on-device Whisper

Runs on the Apple Neural Engine via WhisperKit. Your voice never touches a server. Works fully offline, no account, no bill.

Track 02

Live preview pill

A floating pill shows your words as you speak, so you see the transcription before it lands. Release the key and it pastes at the cursor.

Track 03

Learns your words

A custom dictionary plus corrections it remembers. Product names, teammates, jargon: teach it once and it types them right.

Track 04

Snippets & cleanup

Say a trigger phrase, get your saved snippet. Filler removal and punctuation cleanup are built in. Your clipboard is restored after pasting.

Track 05

13 languages

Auto-detected or pinned. Speak Hindi and get Roman script, or have it translated to English. Code-switch mid-sentence, it keeps up.

Track 06

Stats & history

Every dictation saved and searchable, with your words-per-day stats. Per-app disable and sound cues keep it polite where you want quiet.

Meetings

The minutes write themselves.

OpenWispr captures your mic and the call's system audio, labels who said what, and saves the transcript automatically.

  • You / Others speaker labels, from mic vs system audio. Works with Zoom, Meet, Teams, anything that makes sound.
  • Autosaves every transcript as Markdown, locally.
  • Optional AI summary: local via Ollama, or bring your own key. Skip it entirely and nothing ever leaves the Mac.

Install

Running in three steps.

01

Paste one line

Downloads the app and the Whisper model, installs to /Applications.

curl -fsSL https://openwispr.vercel.app/install.sh | sh
02

Grant two permissions

Microphone to hear you, Accessibility to type for you. macOS asks on first run; that's it for dictation.

03

Hold fn. Speak.

Release, and clean text lands at your cursor. Change the key to any modifier in settings.

Prefer the DMG? Download it, drag to Applications. On macOS 15+ Gatekeeper may block the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Once.
FAQ

Fair questions.

I clicked the app and nothing happened?

OpenWispr lives in your menu bar (microphone icon, top right) — there is no Dock icon. Clicking the app in Applications opens its home window; the menu-bar icon's "Open OpenWispr" does the same.

Why does macOS warn me when I first open it?›
OpenWispr isn't notarized through a paid Apple Developer account, so Gatekeeper flags it like any unsigned open-source app. The install one-liner handles this for you. For the DMG: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, one time only. The code is public on GitHub if you'd rather read it first.
How big is the download?›
The app itself is about 2 MB. On first run it downloads the Whisper model, roughly 1.5 GB, once. After that everything runs locally with no further downloads.
Does any audio or text leave my Mac?›
No. Transcription runs on-device via WhisperKit. There's no account, no telemetry, no server. The only network calls are the one-time model download and, if you opt in, your own AI endpoint (local Ollama or a key you bring).
Do I need Ollama?›
No. Ollama is optional, only for AI meeting summaries. Dictation, filler removal, dictionary, snippets, and transcripts all work without it.
What permissions does it need, and why?›
Microphone to hear you. Accessibility to paste text at your cursor. For meeting transcription, screen & system audio recording so it can capture the other side of the call. Nothing else, and each is requested only when the feature needs it.
