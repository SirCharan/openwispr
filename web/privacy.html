Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

OpenWispr is built so your voice stays on your Mac. This page says what little does leave it. Operator: Charandeep Kapoor, an individual, Bengaluru, India.

What stays on your Mac

Audio, transcripts, meeting notes, summaries, dictionary and history are processed and stored locally. We do not receive them and have no account system.

What is sent, and to whom

  • License checks. When you activate, validate or deactivate a key, the app sends the key and your Mac's name to Dodo Payments' license service.
  • Purchase. Dodo Payments collects your name, email, billing details and payment information at checkout. We receive what Dodo shares for a sale (such as your email and order). See Dodo's privacy policy.
  • Updates. The app fetches an update feed from openwispr.vercel.app. Vercel may keep standard server logs such as IP address.
  • Optional AI providers. Only if you enable one with your own API key, text you ask it to clean or summarize goes to that provider. Local options (Apple on-device, Ollama) send nothing.
  • Website. The website has no analytics, no tracking scripts and sets no cookies. To display the pages, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), and the home page also loads the GSAP animation library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. These requests send your IP address and browser details to Google and Cloudflare. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as IP address.

We do not

  • Sell your data.
  • Upload your audio.

Your choices

Delete your data by deleting the OpenWispr folder in Documents and the app's settings. To ask about data we hold from a purchase, write to charandeepkapoor3@gmail.com.

Retention and children

Purchase records are held by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle them. The site and the app are not directed at children.
